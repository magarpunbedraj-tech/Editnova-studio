Everything a short film needs.

Built for vlogs, shop promos, event videos and school projects. Simple enough for your first edit, careful enough that it never damages your files.

Trim and split
Cut away the shaky start, split a clip at the playhead with one key, and drag clips into the order that tells your story.
Fades and volume
Fade in from black, fade out at the end, mute a noisy clip or boost a quiet voice up to 200%.
Titles in English and Nepali
Press T and type. Put text at the top, middle or bottom, pick a size and colour, and it fades in and out on its own. Devanagari works.
Music that steps aside
Drop in an MP3 and it sits on its own track. When someone speaks in your clip, the music gets quieter automatically.
Export for any screen
MP4 that plays everywhere: 1080p and 4K landscape, vertical for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, square and 4:5 posts.
Fast on NVIDIA graphics
If your laptop has an NVIDIA GPU, exports use it automatically and finish several times faster.
Your originals stay untouched
Edits are instructions saved in a small project file. The video files you imported are never changed or moved.
Recovers after a crash
EditNova keeps a recovery copy of your latest edits, so a power cut costs you seconds, not an afternoon.
Opens almost any video
MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI and M4V from phones, cameras and screen recorders. Mixed sizes are fitted with black bars, never stretched.
Works offline
No sign-in and no internet needed to edit. The only time it goes online is a quiet check for a newer version.

Installed in a minute.

One file installs everything, including the video engine. You don't need administrator rights.

  1. Download

    Click Download for Windows and save EditNova-Studio-Setup.exe.

  2. Run the installer

    Double-click it. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info then Run anyway. This appears because the app is new and not yet code-signed.

  3. Start editing

    Open EditNova Studio from the Start menu, click Import videos, and press Space to play.

Private by design.

EditNova has no account system, no analytics and no cloud. Your clips, projects and exports stay on your drives.

The only network request is a version check against GitHub when the app starts. It sends nothing about you or your work.

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. EditNova Studio is open source under the MIT license. There is no trial, no paid tier and no watermark on your videos.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows SmartScreen warns about apps that are new or not signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info and then Run anyway. You can compare the file's SHA-256 checksum with the one listed on the release page.

A video won't play in the preview. What do I do?

Select its clip and choose Build smooth preview. EditNova makes a lightweight copy just for previewing. Your export always uses the original file at full quality.

Where are my projects saved?

Wherever you choose. A project is a small .novaproject file that remembers your cuts and points to your video files. If you move the videos, open the project and use Replace or relink source on the affected clip.

Can I add text and music?

Yes. Press T to add a title at the playhead and M to add a song. Transitions between clips and speed changes are next on the roadmap.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The first release is for Windows. The code is cross-platform, so other systems may follow.
