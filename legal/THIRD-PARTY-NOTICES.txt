FFmpeg
------
The Windows installer includes ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe, unmodified builds of
FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org) produced by the FFmpeg-Builds project
(https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds). These builds are licensed under the
GNU General Public License version 3 because they include libx264 and other GPL
components. EditNova Studio runs them as separate programs; they are not linked
into EditNova.

* The full license text is in FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt in this folder.
* FFMPEG-SOURCE.txt names the exact build that was included and where its
  complete corresponding source code can be obtained.
* You may replace ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe in the install folder with any
  other compatible build.
